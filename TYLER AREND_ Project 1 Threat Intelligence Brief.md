**Part D: Notable Attack \- A Real Example**

Find ONE real-world attack this group is known for and describe it below.

| Name of the Incident / Attack |
| :---- |
| *Project Chanology (2008)* |

| What Happened? (Write 3-5 sentences in your own words) |
| :---- |
| *In 2008, the Church of Scientology issued a cease-and-desist against the gossip blog Gawker after the blog posted a video of Tom Cruise praising the religion. As a result, users on 4chan began prank-calling the church, sending black faxes, and launching DDoS attacks against their websites. By using tools such as Gigaloader, JMeter, and LOIC, these 4chan users were able to flood the servers with TCP and UDP packets. As a part of the attack, users recorded and uploaded a YouTube video, addressing the leaders of the Church of Scientology, which gained hundreds of thousands of views over 10 days. The popularity of this video and their attack led to a global protest of the Church of Scientology across 43 countries and created the collective hacktivist group “Anonymous”.*  |
